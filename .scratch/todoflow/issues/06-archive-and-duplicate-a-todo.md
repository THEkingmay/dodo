# 06: Archive and duplicate a todo

**What to build:** Two lifecycle shortcuts from the TOR — putting a todo away, and copying one.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Toasts and undo/redo

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A todo can be archived and unarchived
- [ ] An archived todo leaves every active view and appears only in the archived view
- [ ] An archived todo cannot be edited or completed
- [ ] Archiving is manual only — nothing is ever archived automatically after being completed
- [ ] Duplicating copies the title, description, priority and tags
- [ ] The copy is titled with a "(copy)" suffix, is not completed, and carries no due date
- [ ] The copy appears directly below the original
- [ ] Both actions are undoable
